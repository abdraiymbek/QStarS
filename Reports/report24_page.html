QStarS · Report 24 · night of 21–22 October 2026

Under the stall

The 167-radius cool CMFGEN closure had stalled at half its starting residual. One night of measurement found why. The solver had been breaking exact conservation laws, fitting the hydrogen front with directions from the deep layers, and moving a temperature it was meant to hold fixed. Its full Newton steps produced infrared masers with no physical solution.

Four findings

What was under the stall

Cause · fixed

Conservation was broken

34× worst charge imbalance

The step limiter clips each population's change on its own, which breaks the exact number- and charge-conservation equations. Nothing restored them. Helium drifted up to 8× and hydrogen 2.3×, in every Report 23 solve.

Cause · open at the H front

Distant directions as blunt instruments

172 coefficient of a deep-layer direction

The least squares fitted the hydrogen front with a deep-layer direction, whose tiny far-field action the nearly singular front blocks amplify. 97 % of its effect landed at the front; the deep layers took the damage.

Cause · fixed

Fixed T was not fixed

0.50× trial T at the inner boundary

In the fixed-temperature stage, T was still a variable held by a weak pin row. The fit moved it (coefficient 358), and a full trial halved T at depth 166. T is now removed from every direction.

Cause · diagnosed

Trial steps made masers

ρ = 1.063, 1.058 spectral radius

Full steps inverted H I infrared lines at the He front. Where scattering exceeds total opacity, the transfer operator's spectral radius passes 1: no steady radiation field exists, and CMFGEN fails.

Where the residual lives

The fronts improved while the deep interior was damaged

Fixed preconditioned measure ‖W M⁻¹R‖ (one reference preconditioner for every state), split by depth region. The transferred start had a converged deep interior (0.2 and 0.1). By iterate 4 those regions read 66 and 30, while the hydrogen fronts fell from 350 to 78.

Conservation

Helium and charge drifted at the inserted depths

The 167-radius grid interleaves the 84-radius one; the even depths were inserted. From iterate 2 the steps halved n_e and He II at even depths of the deep interior every iterate, the limiter's own factor.

The native failures

A maser with no steady state

In the failed full-step trial, the H I 12→8 line (0.0285 × 10¹⁵ Hz) is inverted at depths 60, 62 and 64. CMFGEN's negative-opacity floor keeps the opacity positive but below electron scattering there. Build 36 added a Newton solve of the scattering fixed point, and the dumped Jacobian answers the question: spectral radius 1.063, 1.058, a fixed point that is negative at depths 57–67. No transfer solver can fix that state; the step must not make it.

Preconditioner

Flooring the singular values

Each equilibrated diagonal block keeps its singular vectors; singular values below 1 % of the largest are raised to that floor. Measured against exact Jacobian products at eight archived states.

Constrained restart

Stage 1 with exact conservation

Projection cannot rescue the damaged iterate-4 state: the projected base alone measured 171 against 118, because its populations had adapted to totals off by up to 8× (He) and 33× (n_e). So the solve restarted from iterate 1, the first accepted state, with the floor, the projection and T held fixed. Every accepted state conserves H, He and charge to 1e-15. The first three iterates accepted full Newton steps and the fixed measure fell from 224 to 128. At the fourth, the full and half steps met supercritical masers again, and the quarter step the driver accepted is worse in the fixed measure (140). A second measure with no inverse, the row-scaled residual, went 2.32 → 1.39 → 1.69 → 2.60 → 2.85. Both must reach zero at the solution: this is a solve that keeps the constraints, not yet a converging one. The run stopped at 05:16, at the end of the session, and can be resumed from its best base.

Next

The narrowest remaining blocker

The constrained solve conserves everything exactly, but each step still damages the converged deep interior. The hydrogen ionization front is so ill-conditioned in the preconditioner that a 22 % far-field radiative coupling of deep-layer directions becomes 99.6 % of their preconditioned action, and the fit uses it.

  1. Make the hydrogen front's linear solve exact: exact Jacobian columns for the front depths (23–28) from one batched probe pass, so far-field couplings are no longer amplified there.
  2. Then compare a front-local least squares (each direction counted only near its own depths) against the current one on the same products; adopt it only if the true linear residual holds.
  3. Continue the constrained stage 1 to convergence, release T, and only then reopen Gates 1–5.
