# Report 17 — formal-solution transfer replaces the moment propagation; nonlinear stall remains

**Outcome: NUMERICAL BLOCKER, moved.** The Report 16 radiation blocker is removed: the
cool H/He radiation field is now the nonnegative characteristic solution of the
relativistic comoving-frame equation, and no released pass produced a negative or
non-finite J. The released population/temperature iteration does **not** converge.

- First it stalls on He II trace-level step control (§4.1–4.2).
- Then the Newton step diverges, whether it uses the native moment linearization or a
  local formal-ALI operator (§4.3–4.4).
- The measured cause is that the local response becomes singular on trapped resonance
  lines (H I Lyα, He I 584, He II Lyα).

The narrowest blocker is the missing nonlocal tangent response of the formal transfer.
No converged cool closure, energy/electron gate, resolution pair, validated nonlocal
Jacobian, self-selected wind or production mdot is delivered. QStarS remains **0.1
development, not v1**.

Status: **final** for this campaign. One damped inexact-Newton test (§4.5) was still
running at finalization; its outcome does not change the gate table unless it converges
*and* then passes every gate.

## 1. Independent audit of the Report 16 diagnosis

Evidence: [`Reports/evidence/formal_ali_20261001/audit.json`](evidence/formal_ali_20261001/audit.json),
produced by `scripts/audit_formal_ali.py`.

1. **The Report 16 seed is not the origin of the failure.** In the captured He I-edge
   frame (`seed_analysis_v2/seed.npz`), the formal-ray J at the outer four depths is
   ~1e99 (γr²J units) while the moment J is ~1e2. The frame is a restart from the Report 15
   runaway checkpoint, so its signed forcing is downstream of an already corrupted
   state. The velocity law there is monotone (σ+1 ≥ 0.0101), so per-ray blue-to-red
   transport is well posed.
2. **The true structural cause.** `MOM_JREL_V9` propagates its *own* previous-frequency
   J and H (lag weight `HS` ≈ 0.79–0.91 in the thin outer wind) and takes only K/J and
   N/J from the ray solution. Its RHS contains signed previous-frequency H and EPS terms,
   so no discrete positivity property exists. Report 16's "angular non-realizability" is
   one symptom of this; a better closure cannot fix it.
3. **The fixed-J spectral export is stale.** With `USE_FIXED_J=T`, `QSTARS_SPECTRAL.bin`
   contains stale opacity buffers: n_e from scattering is a constant 1.503e4 cm⁻³ at every
   depth and χ is frequency-independent. Transfer-active exports vary normally. Do not
   use fixed-J exports for transfer analysis.
4. **The native parabolic short characteristic cannot be used inside a scattering
   iteration.** It chooses its interpolation branch from the sign of its result, so
   the formal map is piecewise-linear in the source. An exploratory run showed an exact
   period-2 limit cycle (iterates and Λ* alternating). That smoke directory was deleted;
   only this description remains.
5. **Native moment J from clean states.** In six synthetic regimes (1000–10⁴× radial
   opacity fronts, 200–2000 km/s, 400–4000 frequencies) the native moment sweep stayed
   positive. The negative moment J is therefore not generic; in the real model it appears
   together with the recombination-front structure and corrupted inherited states.

### Answer to the original question

The evidence selects a **hybrid formal-solution/Newton architecture**:

- The radiation **state** must come from positive characteristic transport. This is
  done, and it removed the Report 16 blocker.
- A realizability-preserving moment closure would not help. The defect is the signed
  self-propagated J/H recursion, not the closure.
- The remaining failure is in the **linearization**. Neither the native moment response
  nor a local Λ* gives a usable Newton step for trapped resonance lines in a cool,
  weakly collisional moving atmosphere.

The narrowest demonstrated blocker is therefore: **the Newton system needs the nonlocal
tangent response of the formal comoving-frame transfer**. That means the dependence of
J_ν on η and χ at neighbouring depths and at bluer frequencies at the same depth, through
(I−Λσ)⁻¹. This is the same object the wind eigenvalue problem requires.

Nothing here shows that the cool solution does not exist physically. Every failure has a
numerical mechanism located in the linearization.
